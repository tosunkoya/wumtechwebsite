COMMON QUESTIONS

About AI Visibility

Is this the same as SEO?

It overlaps with SEO but isn't the same discipline. Traditional SEO optimizes for search engine rankings; AI Visibility focuses on the specific information AI systems need to confidently understand, evaluate and recommend a business in a conversational answer.

Can Wumtech guarantee AI will recommend my business?

No — and be cautious of anyone who claims that. What we can do is help you close the information, clarity and trust gaps that make it harder for AI to consider you in the first place.

How long does the diagnostic take?

The free score takes about 60 seconds. The full 90-Day Starter Plan is typically delivered within a few business days of purchase.

What happens after I get my score?

You'll see exactly where your biggest gaps are. From there, the $49 90-Day Plan turns those gaps into a prioritized, plain-language action list.
